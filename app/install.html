Install in 3 steps

The ACGME Case Submitter extension automatically fills and submits your saved cases to the ACGME portal. One file download and two clicks.

⬇ Download Extension
acgme-case-submitter.zip · Downloads automatically
Setup Steps
1
Unzip acgme-case-submitter.zip
Find acgme-case-submitter.zip in your Downloads folder and unzip it. You will get a single folder ready to load directly into Chrome. ⚠ Move this folder somewhere permanent (Documents or Desktop) before loading into Chrome. If you move or delete it later, the extension will stop working.
🗜 acgme-case-submitter.zip → 📁 acgme-case-submitter/ ← load this into Chrome
2
Open Chrome Extensions
Click the button below to copy the URL, paste it in your Chrome address bar, then click Load unpacked and select the acgme-case-submitter folder.
📁 acgme-case-submitter/
→
Drop here or
click Load unpacked
Paste it in your Chrome address bar and press Enter
3
Pin to toolbar
Click the 🧩 puzzle piece in your Chrome toolbar, find ACGME Case Submitter, and click the 📌 pin icon to keep it visible.

✓ You're all set!

Log into ACGME, open your Case Log app, export your cases as JSON, then click the extension icon in Chrome and upload the file.
